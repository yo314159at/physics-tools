What happens to outgoing infrared radiation as greenhouse gases increase?

Show the answer

More is absorbed and re-emitted in the atmosphere, warming the surface.

  • Visible sunlight warms the surface.
  • Infrared radiation carries heat upward.
  • Greenhouse gases absorb infrared.
Common mistake. Greenhouse gases do not create heat; they slow heat loss.
$$P_\text{out}=\epsilonσT^4,\qquad T\approx T_0+\Delta T_\text{greenhouse}$$
  1. Set greenhouse gas concentration low.
  2. Increase it to high and compare temperature.
  3. Follow an infrared photon upward.
