How does greater tension change wave speed?

Show the answer

Wave speed increases because $v=\sqrt{T/μ}$.

  • Amplitude is perpendicular displacement.
  • Higher frequency means shorter wavelength at fixed speed.
  • Damping reduces amplitude along the string.
Common mistake. The string pieces move transverse to the string, while the disturbance travels horizontally.
$$v=\sqrt{T/μ},\qquad v=fλ$$
  1. Double frequency and observe wavelength.
  2. Increase tension while keeping frequency fixed.
  3. Try fixed, loose, and open end behavior.
