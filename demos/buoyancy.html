What condition determines whether an object floats?

Show the answer

It floats when average density is less than the fluid or the displaced-fluid buoyancy can balance its weight.

  • Buoyant force equals displaced-fluid weight.
  • Heavier fluid provides more buoyancy.
  • Floating objects displace their own weight.
Common mistake. Buoyancy is not caused only by pressure upward; it comes from pressure increasing with depth.
$$F_b=ρ_fV_\text{sub}g,\qquad F_g=mg$$
  1. Change fluid density.
  2. Lower the object and identify its submerged fraction.
  3. Compare floating and sinking conditions.
