Does diffusion require mixing particles to stop moving?

Show the answer

No. Equilibrium is dynamic: particles keep moving, with equal average concentrations.

  • Removal of the divider starts mixing.
  • Random motion erases concentration differences.
  • Equal concentration remains dynamic.
Common mistake. Diffusion is not particles choosing to move toward empty space.
$$∂c/∂t=D∂^2c/∂x^2,\qquad \langle x^2\rangle=2Dt$$
  1. Start with one species only.
  2. Remove the divider and wait.
  3. Compare left and right concentration at equilibrium.
