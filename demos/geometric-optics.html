An object is beyond 2F. Is the image real or virtual?

Show the answer

A converging lens makes a real, inverted, diminished image between F and 2F.

  • Parallel rays pass through the focal point.
  • A ray through the lens center continues straight.
  • Moving the object changes image size and position.
Common mistake. An image may form where rays converge, even if no screen is placed there.
$$1/f=1/d_o+1/d_i,\qquad m=-d_i/d_o$$
  1. Place the object outside 2F.
  2. Move it inside F and describe the virtual image.
  3. Change focal length and compare.
