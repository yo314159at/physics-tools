Double one mass. What happens to the force on both bodies?

Show the answer

Both forces double; action and reaction are equal and opposite.

  • Force grows with each mass.
  • Force follows an inverse-square law with distance.
  • The two forces are equal in magnitude.
Common mistake. A larger mass does not experience a larger gravitational force in a two-body pair.
$$F=G\frac{m_1m_2}{r^2},\qquad G=6.67\times10^{-11}\ \text{N·m²/kg²}$$

  2. Halve the separation; how many times larger is $F$?
  3. Set both masses to 10 kg and compare them.
