Which oscillator responds most at a fixed driving frequency?

Show the answer

The oscillator whose natural frequency is closest to the driving frequency.

  • Large amplitudes occur near natural frequency.
  • Damping limits peak amplitude.
  • Different oscillators resonate at different frequencies.
Common mistake. Resonance is not “any forcing”; it is efficient energy transfer near a natural mode.
$$A(ω)=\frac{F_0/m}{√{(ω_0^2-ω^2)^2+(bω/m)^2}}$$
  1. Scan frequencies from 0.5 to 3 Hz.
  2. Match a driving frequency to one oscillator.
  3. Add damping and compare peak width.
