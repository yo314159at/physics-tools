Does thicker wire have more or less resistance?

Show the answer

Less resistance, because $R=ρL/A$.

  • Resistance grows with length.
  • Resistance grows with resistivity.
  • Resistance falls as area grows.
Common mistake. Area is not length; doubling area doubles the number of current paths.
$$R=ρL/A$$
  1. Double length.
  2. Double area.
  3. Switch to a higher-resistivity material.
