Does increasing plate area increase capacitance?

Show the answer

Yes: $C=\epsilon A/d$.

  • Capacitance rises with area.
  • Capacitance rises as separation shrinks.
  • Charge rises with voltage at fixed capacitance.
Common mistake. Separation affects capacitance, not stored charge directly at fixed voltage.
$$C=\frac{εA}{d},\qquad Q=CV,\qquad U=\tfrac12CV^2$$
  1. Double plate area and record $C$.
  2. Halve separation and compare charge.
  3. Set voltage zero and predict stored energy.
