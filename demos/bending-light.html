At what angle does light from denser to less dense material undergo total internal reflection?

Show the answer

Beyond the critical angle $\theta_c=\sin^{-1}(n_2/n_1)$.

  • Refraction changes direction but not frequency.
  • Higher refractive index usually means slower light speed.
  • Total internal reflection occurs only in the denser medium.
Common mistake. The normal is perpendicular to the interface, not to the incident ray.
$$n_1\sin θ_1=n_2\sin θ_2,\qquad v=c/n$$
  1. Set n1=1.50 and n2=1.00.
  2. Increase the angle until no refracted ray remains.
  3. Measure the critical angle.
