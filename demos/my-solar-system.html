How does changing a planet's mass affect a star?

The star accelerates more when pulled by a larger planet.

  • All bodies attract all other bodies.
  • Trails reveal momentum and escape.
  • The center of mass need not be stationary visually.
Common mistake. A stationary star is only an approximation when a planet is much less massive.
