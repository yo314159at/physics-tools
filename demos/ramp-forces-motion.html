Which force component pulls a block down a ramp?

Show the answer

$mg\sinθ$ along the ramp.

  • Normal force is perpendicular to the ramp.
  • Static friction can prevent motion.
  • Net acceleration depends on component and friction difference.
Common mistake. Gravity does not act along the ramp; it points vertically downward.
$$F_\parallel=mg\sin θ,\qquad F_\perp=mg\cos θ,\qquad a=g(\sin θ-μ_k\cos θ)$$
  1. Set angle 30° and friction zero.
  2. Add friction and find when motion stops.
  3. Compare normal force to $mg$.
