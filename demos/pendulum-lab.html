Does a heavier bob swing with the same period as a lighter bob?

Show the answer

Yes, for the ideal pendulum the period is independent of mass.

  • Length strongly changes the period.
  • Maximum angle gives maximum potential energy and minimum speed.
  • Damping reduces the swing amplitude.
Common mistake. A larger angle does not make a small-angle pendulum take a vastly different time when angles remain small.
$$T=2π√(L/g),\qquad \ddot θ=-\frac gL\sin θ-\frac b m\dot θ$$

The simulation integrates the nonlinear equation, so large-angle periods are also represented.
