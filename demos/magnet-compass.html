Outside a bar magnet, which way do field lines point?

Show the answer

From north pole to south pole outside the magnet.

  • A compass needle is a small magnet.
  • Opposite poles attract.
  • Field strength decreases away from the poles.
Common mistake. The geographic north is near a magnetic south pole, which is why compass north points there.
$$\vec F=q\vec v×\vec B,\qquad \vec τ=\vec μ×\vec B$$
  1. Reverse the magnet.
  2. Place compasses near each pole.
  3. Compare field direction on the equator of the magnet.
