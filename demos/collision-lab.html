When two carts collide, when is momentum conserved? When is kinetic energy conserved?

Show the answer

Momentum is conserved in any isolated collision. Kinetic energy is conserved only in a perfectly elastic collision.

  • Momentum has sign and direction.
  • Elasticity changes the post-collision speeds.
  • The total momentum remains constant if no external impulse acts.
Common mistake. Momentum conservation does not mean the objects stop or have equal speeds.
$$m_1u_1+m_2u_2=m_1v_1+m_2v_2$$

For perfectly elastic motion in one dimension:

$$v_1=\frac{(m_1-m_2)u_1+2m_2u_2}{m_1+m_2},\quad v_2=\frac{(m_2-m_1)u_2+2m_1u_1}{m_1+m_2}$$
