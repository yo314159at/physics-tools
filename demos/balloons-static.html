Why does a charged balloon stick to a neutral wall?

Show the answer

Charges in the wall polarize: induced attraction is stronger than repulsion at distance.

  • Rubbing transfers electrons.
  • Induction separates charges in neutral matter.
  • Attraction can occur after polarization.
Common mistake. A neutral wall is not uncharged everywhere; it remains neutral overall while charges redistribute.
$$F=k_e\frac{|q_1q_2|}{r^2},\qquad q_\text{wall}=0\text{ overall}$$
  1. Rub the balloon and count transferred charges.
  2. Bring it near the wall.
  3. Move it near paper pieces.
