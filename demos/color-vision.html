What color is produced by equal red and green light?

Show the answer

Yellow.

  • Additive mixing uses red, green, and blue primary light.
  • Equal full-strength RGB is white.
  • Filters remove wavelengths rather than add them.
Common mistake. Pigment mixing and light mixing follow different rules.
$$RGB=(r,g,b),\qquad |color|_\text{luminance}\approx0.2126r+0.7152g+0.0722b$$
  1. Set red and green to full, blue to zero.
  2. Add blue and predict the result.
  3. Dim green and describe the hue.
