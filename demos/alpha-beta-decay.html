After one half-life, what fraction remains undecayed?

Show the answer

One-half.

  • Alpha decay lowers $Z$ by 2 and $A$ by 4.
  • Beta-minus decay changes a neutron into a proton.
  • Radioactive decay is random for individuals but exponential for populations.
Common mistake. Half-life means individual nuclei do not each wait exactly one half-life; probabilities decay exponentially.
$$N(t)=N_0e^{-λt},\qquad T_{1/2}=\frac{\ln2}{λ}$$
  1. Start with 1000 nuclei and one half-life.
  2. Compare after two half-lives.
  3. Change decay mode and daughter atomic number.
