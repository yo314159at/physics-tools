Where is the electric field strongest near a point charge?

Show the answer

Closest to the charge, because $E=k|q|/r^2$.

  • Electric field points away from positive charge and toward negative charge.
  • Equipotential lines follow equal voltage.
  • Field strength decreases with distance.
Common mistake. Equipotential lines are not the same as field lines.
$$\vec E=k\sum_iq_i\frac{\vec r-\vec r_i}{|\vec r-\vec r_i|^3},\qquad V=k\sum_i\frac{q_i}{r_i}$$
  1. Place equal positive and negative charges.
  2. Move a test charge and compare forces.
  3. Find an equipotential point on the axis.
