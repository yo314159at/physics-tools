Does faster magnet motion induce more voltage or less?

Show the answer

More: emf is proportional to the rate of change of flux.

  • Voltage appears while flux changes.
  • Reversing direction reverses induced voltage.
  • More coil turns increase emf.
Common mistake. A stationary magnet in a coil produces no induced emf, even if it is strong.
$$\mathcal E=-N\frac{dΦ_B}{dt},\qquad Φ_B=BA\cos θ$$
  1. Move the magnet quickly through the coil.
  2. Reverse direction and compare sign.
  3. Increase coil turns and compare voltage.
