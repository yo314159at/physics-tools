A block has ρ=750 kg/m³. Will it float in water?

Show the answer

Yes. Its density is less than water's 1000 kg/m³.

  • Density combines mass and volume.
  • Large volume and small mass do not make a block heavy.
  • Water density is a useful comparison.
Common mistake. Heavier means denser only when volume is the same.
