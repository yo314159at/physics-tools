What is constant in ideal projectile motion?

Show the answer

Horizontal velocity and vertical acceleration are constant.

  • x and y motions are independent.
  • Velocity is tangent to the path.
  • Acceleration points downward.
Common mistake. The object is not accelerated horizontally in ideal motion.
$$x=v_xt,\qquad y=v_yt-\tfrac12gt^2,\qquad \vec a=(0,-g)$$
  1. Set equal x and y components.
  2. Scrub to maximum height.
  3. Add horizontal acceleration and compare.
