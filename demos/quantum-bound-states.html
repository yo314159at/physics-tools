Why are bound-state energies quantized?

Show the answer

The wavefunction must satisfy boundary conditions at the well walls.

  • Ground state is n=1.
  • Each level has one more node.
  • Probability density is $|\psi|^2$.
Common mistake. The wavefunction itself is not the probability; its squared magnitude is.
$$-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\psi''+V\psi=E\psi,\qquad P=|\psi|^2dx$$
  1. Change well width.
  2. Count nodes in each eigenstate.
  3. Compare density for n=1 and n=3.
