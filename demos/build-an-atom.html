What determines which element an atom is?

Show the answer

The proton count.

  • Protons determine atomic number and element.
  • Neutrons determine isotope mass number.
  • Electron count determines charge.
Common mistake. Neutrons do not determine elemental identity.
$$Z=p,\qquad A=p+n,\qquad q=(p-e)e$$
  1. Build 6 protons, 6 neutrons, and 6 electrons.
  2. Add an electron and read the charge.
  3. Change neutron count and identify the isotope.
