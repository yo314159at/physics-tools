What path difference gives destructive interference?

Show the answer

An odd half-integer multiple of wavelength: $(m+\tfrac12)λ$.

  • Interference depends on phase difference.
  • Equal path differences give constructive interference.
  • Two coherent sources produce a stable pattern.
Common mistake. Waves cancel locally only for a moment if they have opposite phase; energy is not destroyed overall.
$$Δr=mλ\quad\text{constructive},\qquad Δr=(m+\tfrac12)λ\quad\text{destructive}$$
