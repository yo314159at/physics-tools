Why does the least-squares line minimize squared residuals?

Show the answer

Squaring makes every residual positive and penalizes large errors more strongly.

  • Residual is observed minus fitted value.
  • Least squares balances positive and negative residuals.
  • Outliers can strongly affect the fit.
Common mistake. The best-fit line does not necessarily pass through any data point.
$$\chi^2=\sum_i(y_i-(mx_i+b))^2$$
  1. Change slope until squared error falls.
  2. Add a curved model to linear data.
  3. Find residual signs above and below the fit.
