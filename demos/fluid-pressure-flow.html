What happens to pressure with depth?

Show the answer

It increases linearly: $P=P_0+ρgh$.

  • Pressure in a static fluid depends on depth.
  • Narrow pipe areas increase flow speed.
  • Pressure and speed trade with pipe geometry in ideal flow.
Common mistake. A faster fluid is not always at lower pressure in a pump-driven system.
$$P=P_0+ρgh,\qquad A_1v_1=A_2v_2,\qquad P+\tfrac12ρv^2+ρgh=\text{constant}$$
  1. Double depth and compare pressure.
  2. Halve pipe area and compare speed.
  3. Identify a pressure gauge.
