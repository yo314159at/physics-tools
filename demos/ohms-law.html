If resistance doubles while voltage stays fixed, what happens to current?

Show the answer

It halves.

  • Current is proportional to voltage.
  • Current is inversely proportional to resistance.
  • Voltage drops across the resistor.
Common mistake. Resistance does not consume voltage; it produces a voltage drop as current flows through it.
$$V=IR,\qquad I=\frac VR,\qquad P=IV$$
  1. Set $V=12$ V and $R=6$ Ω.
  2. Double $R$ while keeping $V$ fixed.
  3. Calculate power before and after.
