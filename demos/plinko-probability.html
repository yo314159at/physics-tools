For a symmetric Plinko board, which bin receives the most balls?

Show the answer

The center bin, because the most right/left sequences lead there.

  • Each peg deflects left or right by chance.
  • Many paths lead to the center bins.
  • The distribution approaches a normal shape.
Common mistake. The center is not guaranteed for every small sample.
$$P(k)=\binom nkp^k(1-p)^{n-k}$$

For $p=\tfrac12$, the mean is $np$ and the standard deviation is $\sqrt{np(1-p)}$.

  1. Drop 100 balls and compare to binomial probabilities.
  2. Reset and ask whether a single sample repeats.
  3. Increase the row count and notice the peak shape.
