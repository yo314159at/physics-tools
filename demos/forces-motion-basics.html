If two pullers tug equally in opposite directions, what happens to the cart?

Show the answer

The net force is zero, so the cart keeps its current velocity. Zero net force does not necessarily mean zero motion.

  • Acceleration points in the direction of the net force.
  • Friction opposes motion or the attempted motion.
  • Doubling the mass halves the acceleration for the same net force.
Common mistake. The applied force is not always the net force; friction must be included.
$$F_\text{net}=\sum F,\qquad a=\frac{F_\text{net}}m,\qquad f_k=\mu_kmg$$
