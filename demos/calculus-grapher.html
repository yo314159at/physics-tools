Where is the derivative of $x^2$ positive?

Show the answer

For $x>0$; it is negative for $x<0$ and zero at $x=0$.

  • Derivative is slope.
  • Integral graph records accumulated area.
  • Zeros of derivative occur at maxima and minima.
Common mistake. Integrating a derivative recovers the function only up to a constant.
$$f'(x)=df/dx,\qquad F(x)=\int f(x)\,dx$$
  1. Use sine and identify derivative extrema.
  2. Use a quadratic and find the vertex.
  3. Compare integral shape with accumulated area.
