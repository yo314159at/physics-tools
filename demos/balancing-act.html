Can a 5 kg mass balance a 20 kg mass? Where must each mass sit?

Show the answer

Yes. The 20 kg mass can sit one quarter as far from the pivot as the 5 kg mass.

  • Torque depends on force and perpendicular distance.
  • Moving a mass farther from the pivot increases its torque.
  • Equal torque on opposite sides produces rotational equilibrium.
Common mistake. Equal mass does not always produce balance; distance from the pivot matters too.
$$\tau=rF_\perp=rmg,\qquad \sum\tau=0$$

For this horizontal plank, counterclockwise torque is positive and clockwise torque is negative.
