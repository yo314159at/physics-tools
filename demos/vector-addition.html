Two perpendicular vectors have magnitudes 3 and 4. What is the resultant magnitude?

Show the answer

5, by the Pythagorean theorem.

  • The resultant components add independently.
  • Changing direction changes the sum.
  • Vectors in opposite directions cancel.
Common mistake. Vector magnitudes do not add unless vectors point in the same direction.
$$\vec R=\vec A+\vec B,\qquad R_x=A_x+B_x,\qquad R_y=A_y+B_y,\qquad R=√(R_x^2+R_y^2)$$
  1. Make $\vec A=(3,4)$ and $\vec B=(-3,4)$.
  2. Reverse $\vec B$ and compare the resultant.
  3. Turn components on and identify each sign.
