What happens if orbital speed is set too low?

Show the answer

The object falls toward the central mass and follows a lower, faster elliptical path.

  • Gravity points toward the central mass.
  • Velocity is tangent to the orbit.
  • Near a central mass the speed is larger.
Common mistake. Orbiting objects are not beyond gravity; they continuously fall around the central body.
$$F=\frac{GMm}{r^2},\qquad v_\text{circ}=√(GM/r)$$
