What happens to current in series if resistance doubles?

Show the answer

It halves at fixed source voltage.

  • Current has the same value through series elements.
  • Voltage splits across series resistors.
  • An open circuit stops current.
Common mistake. Current is not used up at a resistor.
$$I=V/R_\text{eq},\qquad R_\text{series}=R_1+R_2$$
  1. Add two equal resistors in series.
  2. Double battery voltage.
  3. Open the switch and read current.
