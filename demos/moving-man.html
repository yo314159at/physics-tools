If the man has constant positive acceleration, can his velocity become negative later?

Show the answer

Yes. If he starts moving left fast enough, positive acceleration first slows him, then reverses his direction.

  • The slope of the position graph is velocity.
  • The slope of the velocity graph is acceleration.
  • A negative velocity means the man is moving left, not that his position is negative.
Common mistake. Position, velocity, and acceleration are different quantities; a flat position graph means zero velocity.
$$x(t)=x_0+v_0t+\tfrac12at^2,\qquad v(t)=v_0+at$$

The model assumes one-dimensional motion with constant acceleration. The track is drawn wider than the visible scene so the man can move through the full run.
