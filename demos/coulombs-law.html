If both charges reverse sign, does the force direction stay the same?

Show the answer

No. The product $q_1q_2$ becomes positive again, so like charges repel in both cases.

  • Opposite charges attract.
  • Like charges repel.
  • Force is proportional to charge product and inverse to separation squared.
Common mistake. The sign of force does not come from which charge is bigger.
$$F=k_e\frac{|q_1q_2|}{r^2},\qquad k_e=8.99×10^9\text{ N·m²/C²}$$
  1. Set opposite signs and halve the separation.
  2. Double $q_1$ and record the force.
  3. Reverse $q_2$ and describe the direction.
