Does increasing temperature move the peak to shorter or longer wavelength?

Show the answer

Shorter wavelength, as Wien's law gives $\lambda_\text{max}=b/T$.

  • The visible band is a small part of the spectrum.
  • Hotter objects emit more total power.
  • The peak shifts left as temperature rises.
Common mistake. Color temperature describes the peak and total spectrum, not a single emitted wavelength.
$$λ_\max T=2.898×10^{-3}\text{ m·K},\qquad P=\sigma AT^4$$
