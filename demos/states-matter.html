What happens to average particle speed when temperature rises?

Show the answer

It increases.

  • Particles remain moving in solids, liquids, and gases.
  • Heating increases motion and separation.
  • Phase changes rearrange interaction strength.
Common mistake. Heat does not create particles or motion from nothing; it transfers energy.
